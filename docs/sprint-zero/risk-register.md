# Risk Register

## Instructions

This is a living document. It must be reviewed and updated at every sprint
boundary or whenever a new risk is identified. Each risk carries a status (OPEN,
MITIGATING, CLOSED), an owner (the agent role accountable for driving the
mitigation), and a review date. When a risk is closed, record the resolution
rationale inline. Any team member may propose a new risk by appending a row; the
QA Engineer is responsible for triage and ensuring completeness.

---
