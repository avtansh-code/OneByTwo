Tagline
Split it. Settle it. Simple.
Share expenses with friends, flatmates & family — the India-first way.
Long description
One By Two makes shared spending effortless. Track who paid for what, split any bill — equally, by shares, percentages or exact rupees — and always know your balance at a glance: you're owed, you owe, or all settled up.

When it's time to square up, we use simplified debts to suggest the fewest payments to clear everyone. Built for India: rupees in lakh/crore formatting, +91 sign-in, and a warm design that's a joy to open.

• Friends & groups for trips, flats and family
• Simplified settle-up with pre-filled amounts
• Monthly spend insights by category
• Reminders, activity feed & a gorgeous dark mode
• Private & secure — your data stays yours
