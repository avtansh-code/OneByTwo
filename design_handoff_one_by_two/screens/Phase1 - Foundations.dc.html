Colour system
A warm, ownable palette tuned for both themes. On-primary text is ink, not white — dark-on-marigold clears AA (5.6:1) where white would fail (2.8:1). Balance colours never overlap brand accents.

Motion principles
Delightful but quiet. 200–300ms, ease-in-out as the default curve, a little spring reserved for the FAB and successful settle. Everything honours prefers-reduced-motion with instant cross-fades.
